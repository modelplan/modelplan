# ModelPlan for Mac — v1 candidate notes

[简体中文](#modelplan-for-mac--v1-候选版说明)

**Draft:** These notes describe the intended v1 candidate. The candidate has not yet been approved or released; availability is subject to final integration and validation.

## Quota Widget

The Quota Widget keeps your available quota close at hand. On a fresh installation it appears at the right edge of your screen; you can move it to another supported edge and change its size and behavior in Settings. Auto-hide is off by default. If you turn it on, the widget can collapse to a small edge tab and expand again when you need it.

Each subscription has a place in the widget. Its rings show the remaining quota for the available usage windows. Hover over a ring to open its card for more detail; the card closes after you move away. Cards are not pinned in v1. Click a ring to request a refresh. Refreshes are subject to the reader's minimum interval, so clicking again does not force extra requests.

Use **Show Quota Widget** from the menu to find it: a hidden widget is shown, a collapsed widget expands temporarily, and an already visible widget is highlighted. The widget's right-click menu also lets you hide it. The menu bar shows the tightest available remaining quota and a problem indicator when attention is needed; open **Settings > Diagnostics** for details.

## Cockpit

The cockpit is the larger view for exploring your subscriptions and published model information. It brings quota details together with ModelPlan's published picks and model comparisons. The candidate cockpit is still subject to its enablement and acceptance gates; current interim builds may not open it.

## Public reset events

When ModelPlan has a verified public announcement about an extra reset, the widget can show an event indicator. An announcement is not proof that your own quota has reset: your rings update from a new reading. Confirmed events and announcements are shown as different states, and events expire when their published or applicable display period ends.

The candidate is intended to include source links on eligible events, such as the original post or a reviewed public source. A link is shown only when an eligible source is available; an event can still appear without one. Link presentation is awaiting integration and validation.

## Privacy and consent

Your local usage and quota data stays on your Mac; ModelPlan does not upload it. Public model information is downloaded separately. Some quota readers need to contact the relevant provider to obtain a reading, and may require a credential or access to an existing local session. You choose which supported readers to allow during setup or in Settings; detecting a reader does not grant it permission. You can change or revoke those choices later. Browser-session access is not included in **Select All**.

## Known limits

- The v1 candidate targets **macOS 15 or later**. The existing beta guide also discusses older beta builds; that does not extend the candidate's support target.
- The current beta has no Apple Developer ID signature and is not notarized. macOS may block the first launch until you approve the app in Privacy & Security. See the [Mac beta installation guide](../install-beta.md) for the current steps.
- Some cockpit features are deferred to v1.1, including history charts, longer-term totals, forecast and amber expiry cues, and historical model ordering. The enlarged live 3D field is planned for after v1.
- The candidate and its exact feature set still require final review and device validation. These notes are not an announcement or a download.

# ModelPlan for Mac — v1 候选版说明

**草稿：**本页描述计划中的 v1 候选版。候选版尚未获批或发布，具体功能以最终集成和验证结果为准。

## 额度挂件

额度挂件让你随时查看剩余额度。全新安装时，挂件显示在屏幕右侧；你可以在设置中将它移到其他受支持的边缘，调整大小和行为。默认关闭自动隐藏；开启后，挂件可以收起为屏幕边缘的小条，需要时再展开。

每个订阅在挂件中都有自己的位置，圆环显示可用用量周期的剩余额度。将鼠标移到圆环上可打开详情卡片，移开后卡片会关闭。v1 不支持固定卡片。点击圆环可请求刷新；刷新受读取器的最短间隔限制，重复点击不会强制增加请求。

在菜单中选择**显示额度挂件**即可找到它：完全隐藏时会显示，收起时会暂时展开，已经显示时会高亮提示。也可以通过挂件的右键菜单将其隐藏。菜单栏显示当前最紧张的剩余额度，出现问题时显示提示；详情见**设置 > 诊断**。

## 驾驶舱

驾驶舱提供更大的视图，用于查看订阅和公开的模型信息，将额度详情、ModelPlan 发布的推荐以及模型对比放在一起。候选版驾驶舱仍须通过启用和验收关卡；当前过渡版可能无法打开。

## 公开重置事件

当 ModelPlan 收到经过验证的公开额外重置公告时，挂件可以显示事件提示。公开公告不代表你的额度一定已经重置：圆环会根据新的读取结果更新。公告和已确认事件显示为不同状态，并在公布的时间窗口或适用的展示期结束后消失。

候选版计划为符合条件的事件提供来源链接，例如原帖或经过审核的公开来源。只有存在符合条件的来源时才显示链接；没有链接的事件仍可能显示。链接展示仍在等待集成和验证。

## 隐私与授权

本机用量和额度数据保留在你的 Mac 上；ModelPlan 不会上传这些数据。公开模型信息单独下载。部分额度读取器需要联系相应服务商获取读数，也可能需要凭据或访问现有的本机会话。你可以在首次设置或设置中选择允许哪些受支持的读取器；检测到读取器并不代表已经授权。之后也可以更改或撤销授权。**全选**不包含浏览器会话访问。

## 已知限制

- v1 候选版面向 **macOS 15 或更高版本**。现有测试版安装指南也提到较早的测试版，这不改变候选版的支持范围。
- 当前测试版没有 Apple Developer ID 签名，也未经 Apple 公证。macOS 可能会阻止首次打开，需要你在「隐私与安全性」中批准该应用。当前步骤请参见 [Mac 测试版安装指南](../install-beta.md)。
- 部分驾驶舱功能推迟到 v1.1，包括历史图表、更长周期的汇总、预测和到期琥珀色提示，以及基于历史记录的模型排序。放大的实时 3D 场景计划在 v1 之后提供。
- 候选版及其确切功能仍需最终审核和设备验证。本页不是发布公告，也不提供下载。
