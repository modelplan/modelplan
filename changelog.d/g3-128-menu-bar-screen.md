With two displays, the attached cockpit and its launch rack always open under the menu bar icon's display, even when the widget's ignition is on the other display, and Put back returns a cockpit dragged to another display home under the icon. On a put back the rack starts unfolding together with the cockpit's flight. A cockpit you just detached comes to the front and takes the keyboard, and stays an ordinary window afterwards. The cockpit now has one size on every display, fitted to the smallest connected display (at most 85 % of its height), so dragging it between displays never resizes it.
