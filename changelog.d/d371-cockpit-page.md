The cockpit porthole HUD no longer has a background panel: one scrim that fades out at every edge, a 1 px dark outline on its text, the ★ effort column aligned across rows, and fewer whole rows when six do not fit the safe area. The Resources column bars and numbers use the normal, amber (from 80 %) and red (from 90 %) status colours with a 3 % hysteresis, and the Resources tab icon loses its small square frame.
The porthole HUD scrim is now the half-opacity variant B (reaching 48 pt to the sides, 16 pt above and below) with the rank numbers in #aab6bf, and the Resources column cell is two fixed layers (number line with the tag inline, 6 pt bar with a 1 px trend inside the track), its side value going full, short, then hidden.
