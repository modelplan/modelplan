The cockpit now fits its size to your screen. A new "Cockpit size" setting offers Auto (the default), 80, 85, 90 and 100 percent; Auto draws the cockpit at 85 percent on laptop screens up to 1512 points wide, such as a MacBook Air, and at 100 percent on larger displays, and switches when the cockpit opens on another screen. On a screen too short for the chosen size, such as a MacBook Air with the Dock at the bottom, the whole cockpit, flames included, shrinks evenly to fit below the menu bar instead of switching to the short version; text stays sharp and controls respond where they are drawn. The short version only appears where the full cockpit would drop below 75 percent.
