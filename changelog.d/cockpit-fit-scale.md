The cockpit now opens at 85 percent of its designed size by default, and a new "Cockpit size" setting (80, 85, 90 or 100 percent) changes it. On a screen too short for the chosen size, such as a MacBook Air with the Dock at the bottom, the whole cockpit, flames included, shrinks evenly to fit below the menu bar instead of switching to the short version; text stays sharp and controls respond where they are drawn. The short version only appears where the full cockpit would drop below 75 percent.
