On a MacBook Air the cockpit now always opens at its full height, flames included: when the screen is too short it shrinks evenly to fit below the menu bar instead of switching to the short version, with sharp text and controls that still respond where they are drawn. The short version only appears on screens where the full cockpit would drop below 75 percent. A new "Cockpit size" setting (Automatic, 100 %, 90 %, 80 %) chooses a fixed size; a size that does not fit a screen uses Automatic there.
