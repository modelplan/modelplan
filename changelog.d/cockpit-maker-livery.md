The cockpit takes every maker colour (engine tabs, fader vendor strip, session list, subscription header, launch-field cards) from one shared maker colour table instead of three disagreeing per-file sets: GLM is no longer purple, Codex is the light neutral and Claude its brand orange. When two makers shown together are too close in colour (computed distance), the later one uses its alternate shade, and on the day theme the marks are drawn darker so they stay readable. Logos and names stay beside every colour.
Black-and-white makers (Codex, GLM) and makers close to a UI colour (Claude near the alert red) are drawn two-tone in the cockpit, the way the website rockets are: the brand colour with an ink trim, so Codex reads as white with black trim and GLM as dark with white trim instead of plain UI white.
