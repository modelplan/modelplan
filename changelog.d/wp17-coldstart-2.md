A click on the menu bar icon, the open-cockpit shortcut or the menu item while the app is still starting now opens the cockpit as soon as the engine is ready, with the icon shown pressed at once, instead of being ignored. The quota widget draws only the face of the edge it rests on (the other edges are drawn when a drag starts), and the app no longer asks the engine twice for the rows and the consent facts at launch, so the first readings arrive sooner.
