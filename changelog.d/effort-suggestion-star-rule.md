The weekly effort hint is now on by default and starts from each model's published ★ effort: it suggests at most one rung up or two rungs down on the same model, from your own quota history and current pace, and stays quiet when a scheduled or officially announced reset is near. It shows a short line without numbers ("Quota to spare · High is affordable" / "Quota running low · try Low"), never suggests another model or Fast, and can be turned off in Settings, with `modelplan history --effort-suggestion off`, or with `MODELPLAN_EFFORT_SUGGESTION=off`.
