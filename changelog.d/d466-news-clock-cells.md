Cockpit: every quota cell now has two short lines beside its number. The first is the real reset time ("resets in 4d14h", narrow "in 4d14h"), always in quiet grey; the second is our estimate ("est. out in 2d16h", narrow "out 2d16h"), amber or red only when it runs out before the reset, otherwise a faint "lasts to reset". Amber now means only that: a quota that will run out before it resets (the old "under 50 %" amber is gone). Forecasts say "est." in English and the Chinese word for estimate in Chinese; "about" is kept for the ≈$ cost only. The alert sentence has one long form (alert list, lamp tooltip, widget card) and one short form (single-line places), and both keep the reset time. A reading whose latest attempt failed keeps its numbers with "Fail·2h"; once its reset time has passed the cell shows "-- no reading" instead. The alert list counts only amber and red alerts; a banked reset expiring within 48 hours is a counted amber line that never lights the lamp, and news, peak times and reading problems are listed below, uncounted. Reset news (a blue dot on the key, a plain blue "+1" in the reserve) follows one news clock; an undated announcement that is never confirmed retires after 48 hours, and a confirmed direct reset clears once the reading shows the window restarted. The All key no longer carries a stale dot. When the app remembers which subscriptions it has read, a subscription gets a column only after its first good reading.
