The engine does almost no work while nothing changes. The app-server keeps one in-memory list of the Claude Code, Codex and ZCode session logs it may read and lists a folder again only when it changed, instead of walking every history folder every few seconds; a new session still appears within 6 seconds, and an older session that resumes within 30 seconds. The 30-second observation pass skips a log whose size and time are as its last pass left them, and it no longer rebuilds the widget's data when it wrote nothing. The observation log moved from `observed/observe-decisions.json` to `diagnostics/observe-decisions.json`, outside the folder the engine watches, and is rewritten only when an observer's outcome changes. Session paths the engine remembers are checked again against their history folder right before they are read, and an observation whose consent changed while it ran is dropped and redone.
