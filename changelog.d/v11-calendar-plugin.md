Cockpit calendar plugin (v1.1): a calendar column shows today's next 36 hours (or tomorrow and the day after) with the published peak hours of your plans in their colours, your own 5-hour and weekly resets, reset cards and announced resets, all in your local time. A click opens a 7-day week view on the MFD screen that you can zoom to about 8 hours; clicking a day there moves the column to that day.
