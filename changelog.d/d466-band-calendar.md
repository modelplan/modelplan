Cockpit: the subscription band drops the Calendar key and column. Keys show the short name ("ZCode", "Codex") and add the plan only when two keys share a name. The Today row leads with tokens as the big figure, then the estimated cost, then speed, and the model cards above the faders show tokens large with the estimated cost on the small line. Each subscription column has a thin 24-hour strip at its right edge (now, peak hours, resets, run-out, timed official resets); hovering it lists what comes next and clicking it opens the new Calendar tab for that subscription. Calendar is now the fifth page of the big screen and follows the Today / 7d / 30d switch: a 24-hour agenda, a week grid with the picked day in words, and a dated list. Items that span several days are a single chip on the day that matters.
