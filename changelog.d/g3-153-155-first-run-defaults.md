First run no longer ticks tools that have nothing to read (no plan, not signed in, a login type or local login data that cannot be read; a timeout or retry stays ticked, and a choice you made never changes), ZCode's QR-code sign-in is offered as its own opt-in (off until you tick it) instead of being called unsupported, and the cockpit's selected date key no longer shows the gold keyboard ring after the window loses and regains focus. The cockpit's subscription columns now show their usage row for the selected range (today, 7 days or 30 days: cost, tokens and speed) and say "No usage" for the range instead of "Quota only" when the history is read but nothing is recorded.
