Settings has a new Plugins pane right after Subscriptions, with the calendar and resource plugin rows on the same grid, a one-line note, and the resource plugin's sampling cadence under its interval control; the plugins are no longer listed on the Subscriptions pane. A plugin ring on the Quota Widget and its hover card now offer Turn Off <plugin>… on right-click, which asks for confirmation (Cancel is the default) and turns the plugin off the same way as its Settings switch.
