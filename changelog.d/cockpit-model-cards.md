The cockpit's model cards above the faders now show how much each model was used rather than its effort ladder: under the subscription-colour edge and the model name comes the estimated cost in large type (the token count until a cost can be estimated, and for models with no public price), a share bar in the subscription colour, the tokens with the model's share of the range ("212M 17%"), and a small effort line ("High ★Med"; just a dim "★Med" when the current effort is the recommended one). The five small ability bars are gone. A model with no use in the range is dimmed and reads "Unused", with its lever dimmed; a model in use without a usage record reads "No record"; an unreported effort reads "Effort ?". Every card line fits in nine characters in English and Chinese. While an effort is being tried the card reads "Try XHigh / Now High" in blue. The current effort is the one with the most activity in the last 15 minutes, held until another clearly leads, and another effort with a notable share gets a small white dot beside the slot. Each card has one session lamp under the row label "Sessions": lit in the subscription colour while a session runs, dim when the model was used in the range. Clicking a card frames it and brings in the effort page over 150 ms; Esc first cancels a trial, whose ghost lever glides back in 280 ms, and a second Esc returns to the previous page; clicking the card again or empty fader space deselects it. Motion follows Reduce Motion.
