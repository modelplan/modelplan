After the cockpit is detached from the launch rack, the nozzles burn at full thrust for about 1.2 s and then fade out completely (about 0.4 s) instead of staying lit while it is parked; with the flame out there is no ember and no subscription glow, and nothing keeps animating, so an idle parked cockpit costs less energy. Returning to the rack still burns full thrust and then settles to the ember, and a cockpit at rest on the rack keeps its ember and glow.
