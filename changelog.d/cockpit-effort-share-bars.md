The cockpit's effort comparison on the big screen is redrawn: two stereo-lane bars, ability (white) and cost (violet), whose full length is the model's own top effort (100 %). The whole 0-100 % track is always drawn with a bright 100 % end stop and notches every 10 % (a full-height one at 50 %). With no trial the bar is one solid fill with its percentage below; a trial splits the bar into two lanes from 0, the real configuration on top and the trial in blue underneath, with a blue percentage (one number with a blue "=" when equal, and a fast value past 100 % pins to the end with a chevron and prints the exact number). The recommended effort is a thin gold line with a small ★ above the bar. Hovering an effort on a bar, the chart or the effort keys previews it on both; hovering the Ability or Cost row sets that chart line apart; clicking a bar segment tries that effort. Below the bars, a line chart shows ability, cost and time for every published effort as a percentage of the top effort. On the small fader screen, fast mode is a symbol-only bolt beside the lamp (white real, blue trial). The old table of ratios is gone.
