The Dock quota card's header no longer reserves two text lines: a title that fits on one line shrinks the header to that line, and the two-line height stays only when the plan line wraps under the name. The card grid's label and percent columns now size to the card's own content (capped at the approved widths), so short rows like Weekly and Reserve no longer leave a wide empty gap before the bar; the ten-segment bar keeps its approved width.
