The widget now has one dot: blue for new reset news (hollow announced, solid confirmed). The amber forecast and reserve-expiry dots and the white credits dot are gone. A reset announcement fades 30 minutes after you look at it (hovering its card, or seeing it on the cockpit's visible page or in the alert list; showing the widget or opening the cockpit is not a look), after 3 hours you are at the Mac (idle, sleep and lock do not count), or 24 hours at most. A banked reset only adds to your reserve. A ring turns amber only when the estimate runs out before the reset, and red at the red line. An amber, red or used-up card ends with the alert sentence again, under a "Next" block (at most 3 lines; left out in an alert with nothing in 24 hours). The menu shows the worst alert in its short form. A subscription gets a ring only after its first good reading on this Mac. The ignition key's tooltip says what a press does: Open, Close or Show cockpit. The cockpit page follows the same news clock, and Calendar is no longer listed under Settings, Plugins (the calendar is a fixed tab).
